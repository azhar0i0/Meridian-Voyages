Why we exist

Planning a good trip shouldn't take three weekends

Meridian Voyages started with forty browser tabs and the nagging feeling something had been missed. So we bundled the whole thing — the flights, the permits, the driver who actually shows up — and put one planner in charge of it end to end.

  • Groups capped at twelve. Small enough to eat where the locals eat, big enough to keep it affordable.
  • Every route is editable. Different dates, another departure city, extra nights — say the word and we re-price it.
  • Solo, couples, families. Prices are per person sharing; single rooms quoted on request.
  • Cover offered on every trip — cancellation, medical, kit and delays.
